Philippe is a tenured professor in the Department of Computer Science and Operations Research (DIRO) at the Université de Montréal (UdeM), where his research focuses on language processing. He earned his PhD in 1995 from the University of Avignon, conducting his research at the LIA laboratory in the field of speech recognition. Prior to this, he spent three years working with the speech research team at IDIAP (the Dalle Molle Institute for Perceptual Artificial Intelligence) in Switzerland. Between 1995 and 1997, Philippe held a position as a temporary teaching and research assistant (ATER) at the University of Avignon. The following year, he completed a postdoctoral fellowship at the CTT—a unit within the Speech, Music and Hearing (TMH) department at the KTH Royal Institute of Technology in Stockholm. Philippe joined the RALI laboratory in 1998, where his work includes research on machine translation. In 2007, he took a sabbatical year to visit ENST, LIMSI, and IRIT.
