Guy Lapalme

Emeritus Professor

Université de Montréal

Since 2019, I have been a professor emeritus at the Université de Montréal. I taught there for 40 years in the Department of Computer Science and Operations Research, working primarily in natural language processing within the RALI laboratory. Until September 2026, I served as the academic lead for the MONA project.
